# Wednesday review checklist

Run every Wednesday (payday). Verify; don't estimate. Commit the review as `weekly-reviews/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## 1. Money in
- [ ] Pay received. Fixed living-costs amount moved out first, the rest per the rules below.
- [ ] Emergency fund (kept outside the portfolio) at its target? If not, refill it first.
- [ ] Cash shield at 15%+? If not, top it up before any buying.

## 2. Verify every holding (core + defensive + sandbox)
For each one: price, % change since last review, news, valuation, dilution, next earnings date.
- [ ] Recompute weights from live prices across **all** accounts (Revolut general, Revolut ISA, Trading 212 ISA).
- [ ] Total exposure per stock across accounts, not per account.

## 3. Limits
- [ ] Core: 5 AI names max · Anchor sleeve: 3 names / 15% max (one in, one out), rising to 4 / 20% only when the switch triggers
- [ ] Each anchor still passes the diversification test (beta < 0.8, corr. NVDA < 0.5, falls less than the Nasdaq in bad weeks)
- [ ] Single stock ≤ 20%
- [ ] Energy/nuclear ≤ 35% · Neocloud ≤ 15%
- [ ] Sandbox ≤ 5%
- [ ] Cash shield ≥ 15%
- [ ] AI-linked share of the book: ____% (know the number; ~70% target means a 30% AI fall costs ~21%)

## 4. Plans committed in advance
- [ ] Drawdown plan: QQQ vs its highest close. Any trigger hit (−15 / −25 / −35%)? → [plan](../plans/2026-10-10-drawdown-plan.md)
- [ ] Concentration plan: phase status → [plan](../plans/2026-10-10-concentration-plan.md)
- [ ] 14 Oct only: GOOGL vs VST/CEG core decision → [decision page](../plans/2026-10-14-decision-googl.md)
- [ ] VST/CEG ratio: above ~0.62 favours CEG, below ~0.48 favours VST, otherwise split new money

## 5. Watchlist and gates
- [ ] UBER gate (4 Nov results) · MCD gate (Q3 results, expected 4 Nov, confirm) · NU gate (Q3 results, mid-Nov) · BWXT gate (Q3 results, Mon 2 Nov after close) · AVGO (January gate)
- [ ] 14 Oct only: run the anchor test on BWXT, plus its correlation with VST (must be < 0.5)
- [ ] Third and fourth anchors: MCD and BWXT, order decided after both report (see the [concentration plan](../plans/2026-10-10-concentration-plan.md))
- [ ] The switch (anchors 15% → 20%, VUKG 6% → ~1%): sleeve full, two good quarters each, every anchor still passing the test? Only then.
- [ ] 2–4 Nov is crowded (BWXT, UBER and MCD results, Phase 2 deadline): judge each gate on its own, buy UBER first
- [ ] Anything new must replace a core name, or go in the sandbox
- [ ] [Watch items](../plans/watch-items.md): any Terafab milestone hit?
- [ ] VST edge check: forward P/E gap to CEG, and VST's correlation with NVDA

## 6. Swing trades
- [ ] Open trades: stop still placed as a real order? Time stop or earnings date within the next week?
- [ ] Close and log any finished trade in [`swing-log.md`](../trades/swing-log.md)

## 7. Write it down
- [ ] Decisions and the reason for each
- [ ] Commit
