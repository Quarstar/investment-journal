## Why I own it
Nvidia is the default platform for AI computing: GPUs, networking and the software ecosystem around them. I treat it as the closest thing to an "AI index" in a single stock. It guided to $108B revenue for its fiscal Q3, with the Rubin platform ramping.

## What has to be true
- [ ] Hyperscaler and AI-lab capex keeps growing
- [ ] Nvidia keeps its share as customers build their own chips
- [ ] Gross margins stay in the 70s

## What would make me sell
- **Thesis break:** two quarters of falling data-centre revenue, or a major customer shifting most of its spend to in-house silicon.
- **Size:** trim back to target if it grows past ~18%.

## Risks I'm watching
- **Custom silicon:** Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium, and now Musk's [Terafab](../plans/watch-items.md). xAI is a large Nvidia customer today.
- **AI financing:** OpenAI's annualised revenue was reported near $50B vs ~$70B previously signalled (Oct 2026), against very large compute commitments.
