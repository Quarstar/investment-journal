# Conviction slot: NBIS vs IREN · 16 Oct 2026

The 5% conviction slot (G+N5) was written for Nebius. This page tests whether IREN is the better name for it. Same thesis (the neocloud bottleneck), same money, same rules. Prices are the 15 Oct close; fundamentals are the latest filings and the stockanalysis.com statistics pages read on 16 Oct.

> Personal record, not financial advice.

## Short answer

**NBIS keeps the slot.** IREN is the cheaper and more beaten-down stock, and if the slot were a pure trade on the sector bouncing it would be the higher-beta way to play it. But the slot is a conviction holding through at least one results cycle, and on the things that decide whether a neocloud survives its own build-out, Nebius is ahead on four of five: contract quality, business purity, profitability of the cloud business, and cost of debt. IREN wins on valuation and dilution per unit of growth is a wash (both are bad). IREN stays a sandbox holding at ~0.9%, scored on its own.

## What would flip it to IREN

1. **The frontier-lab contract is confirmed as Anthropic at scale** (a multi-year, multi-GW deal would put IREN's backlog quality close to Nebius's at a quarter of the valuation).
2. **The mining exit completes** and AI cloud passes half of revenue (then the EV/contracted-ARR gap of 3.3× vs ~7× is comparing like with like).
3. **Financing costs converge**: IREN refinances the 9% Mackenzie debt toward 6%, or Nebius's next raise prices like a non-IG borrower.
4. **The 5 Nov print** shows operating ARR well above $1B with capex funded without more equity.

If two of those four land, the slot is re-battled.

## Decision

- **Conviction slot: NBIS, 5% base, band 3–7%,** as written on 16 Oct.
- **IREN: sandbox holding (~0.9%) from the overridden swing,** no adding; thesis page by 4 Nov; reviewed at the 5 Nov results. Any add would need the 90-day dilution check against the August $2.4B financing and the FY26 convertibles.
- **Both go on the neocloud thesis page** with the same four trackers (backlog, borrowing cost, GPU rental prices, share count) and a kill line each.
