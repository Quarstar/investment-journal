# Watch items

Things that could change a thesis but don't need action yet. Check at each Wednesday review.

## Terafab (Tesla · SpaceX · xAI chip fab, Texas)
**Status (Oct 2026):**
- Launched March 2026 as a $20–25B venture.
- Intel is the main foundry partner (18A/14A processes).
- Musk said on 7 Oct that they'll "build and run the fab" themselves, with TSMC talks "just discussions" about a possible sublease.
- Stated goal: one terawatt of AI compute per year, ~80% of it for space-based AI satellites.
- No official timeline. Estimates point to first chips around late 2028 and volume in 2029.

**Why it matters**
| Holding | Possible effect |
|---|---|
| TSM | Long-term competitor, or a partner if the sublease happens. Musk's own claim that suppliers can't keep up is a demand signal. |
| NVDA | Adds to the shift towards in-house chips (Google, Amazon, Musk). xAI is a large Nvidia customer today. |
| VST | A giant Texas fab needs firm power in Vistra's biggest market. Speculative, no deal. |
| VST / power thesis | If AI compute moves to orbit on solar power, part of the long-run "ground power is the bottleneck" thesis weakens. |

**Milestones that would make me act**
- [ ] Groundbreaking and major tool orders (e.g. ASML)
- [ ] Intel 18A/14A yields shown at volume
- [ ] TSMC sublease agreed or rejected
- [ ] xAI cutting Nvidia orders

**Indirect play to consider (needs a core slot, one in, one out):** fab equipment and materials suppliers win whoever builds the fab, e.g. lithography (ASML) and silicon wafers (Shin-Etsu).

## Anthropic IPO
**Status (12 Oct 2026):** not listed. Filed confidential IPO paperwork with the SEC on 1 Jun 2026. Reports say Nasdaq, with a listing as early as Oct–Nov 2026, but no date is confirmed. Last private valuation ~$965B (Series H, May 2026). Revenue run-rate ~$65B (end of July 2026), not yet profitable.

**Is it an adopter?** Not really. It's an AI lab: one of the biggest *buyers* of compute from the build-out, so it sits between suppliers and adopters. If AI demand disappoints, the labs are at the centre of it. If the build-out overshoots and compute gets cheap, labs benefit.

**Rules if it lists (written before the event):**
- [ ] No buying on listing day or in the first weeks. Wait for its **first quarterly results as a public company**.
- [ ] Valuation check against revenue growth. At $1–2T on ~$65B run-rate, it would be priced at roughly 15–30× sales.
- [ ] Sandbox-sized only (≤ 2%) until it passes a normal gate. A core slot needs one in, one out.
- [ ] Note the lock-up expiry date (insiders can usually sell ~6 months after listing, which often pressures the price).
