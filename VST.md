## Why I own it
AI data centres need firm, around-the-clock power, and nuclear is the cleanest way to supply it at scale. Vistra is the largest competitive power generator in the US and owns a nuclear fleet alongside gas and a retail power business. That gives it more ways to profit from rising power prices than a pure nuclear operator.

**My edge claim:** the market still prices VST as a power producer (~15× forward earnings vs CEG ~20×; weekly correlation with NVDA −0.02 over the past year) rather than as an AI-infrastructure partner. I expect that gap to close as Helix sites come online.

## What has to be true
- [ ] Hyperscalers keep signing long-term contracts for firm power (Constellation–Google, Oct 2026, is the template).
- [ ] Nuclear uprates and life extensions get funded (Vistra's conditional ~$4.2B DoE loan, Oct 2026).
- [ ] Power prices in its markets stay elevated as data-centre demand grows.

## What would make me sell
- **Thesis break:** hyperscalers cut data-centre capex plans or shift to self-built generation.
- **Rates:** VST is capital-intensive and carries debt. A sustained move higher in long yields is a sizing risk, not a sell signal on its own.
- **Size:** trim back to target whenever it drifts above ~13%.
