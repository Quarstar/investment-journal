## The ladder (same for every order)

1. **Step 1, limit:** last price × 1.01, rounded to the tick, placed after 14:45 UK (never in the first 15 minutes), valid to the Friday close of that week. A 1% limit sits inside a normal day's range for all of these names, so it usually fills within a day or two without giving the price away.
2. **Step 2, Monday:** if unfilled, cancel and re-place at Monday's price × 1.005, valid one day.
3. **Step 3, Tuesday:** if still unfilled, market order at 15:00 UK. The point of these sales is to be out, not to top-tick them.
4. **Fractions:** Revolut can't attach a limit to a fractional share, and some apps refuse fractional limits. Rule: whole shares by limit, the fractional remainder by market order once the limit has filled.
5. **Never into results.** Each order's deadline is before the company's next results date.
6. **Clocks:** UK clocks go back on Sun 25 Oct, US clocks on Sun 1 Nov, so in the week of 26 Oct the US session runs 13:30–20:00 UK, not 14:30–21:00.
