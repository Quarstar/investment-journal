## Why I own it
MP owns Mountain Pass in California, the only scaled rare-earth mine and separation plant in the US, and is building the magnet supply chain on top of it. In July 2025 the Department of Defense became its largest shareholder and set a **10-year price floor of $110/kg for NdPr** (the government pays the difference below it, to the end of 2035), with a 10-year offtake for the magnets from the planned "10X" plant. My reasons, 28 Oct: I'd rather keep the name that is on the bigger discount and still in its build-out, because it has a **giant moat**: China controls most of the world's rare-earth processing, and MP is the West's answer with the US government standing behind it.

## What has to be true
- [ ] The government support holds: the price floor and the offtake stay in place, whatever the politics.
- [ ] The magnet build-out stays on schedule: Independence (Texas) deliveries ramp, and 10X stays on its timeline without a large overrun.
- [ ] The business earns more on its own over time. Today it leans on the floor: price-protection income was $17.6M last quarter, without which adjusted EBITDA would be more than 60% lower ([Motley Fool, 1 Nov](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/01/mp-materials-has-collapsed-60-from-its-highs-heres/)).
- [ ] Funding comes mainly from the government deal and debt, not repeated share sales.

## What would make me sell (written before it happens)
- **Thesis break:** the price floor or the offtake is cut, delayed or renegotiated down; or 10X slips by a year or more.
- **Dilution:** the share count up more than 10% in a year.
- **The gate (sandbox rule, 14 Oct):** the 2% stays only if this page exists and the **Thu 5 Nov** print holds. "Holds" means: revenue at or above the ~$117.5–119.0M consensus, magnet milestones reaffirmed, and no cut to the government support. A miss on two of those three sells the rest on the order-sheet ladder (never into the print itself).
- **Take-profit:** the resting **$48 limit** stays as the pre-set exit if it bounces (placed 14 Oct as my bounce level).
- No adding while it's in the sandbox (no averaging down).
