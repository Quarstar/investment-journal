# Neoclouds: NBIS (Nebius) and IREN

**Sleeves:** NBIS is the carry in the weapons sleeve (rules in the [target portfolio](../plans/target-portfolio.md), 22 Oct); IREN is a sandbox holding (no adds) · **Cap:** 15% neocloud combined

> DRAFT, written 4 Nov 2026: rewrite in my own words before publishing as final.

## Why I own them
AI needs GPUs that are racked, powered and rented out, and the hyperscalers can't build fast enough on their own. Neoclouds sell that capacity on multi-year contracts to Microsoft, Meta and the AI labs. My bet is on the bottleneck: whoever has **power secured, GPUs installed and contracts signed** gets paid before the rest of the market catches up. NBIS is my conviction name: my best trade (+84.5%, Mar–Oct 2026), a company that has bounced back from every doubt in the past year. IREN I hold because, in my words (15 Oct), *"it's a company that will grow… once it stops burning cash it'll be a great company."*

## The risk I'm taking on
Both are building faster than they earn, so **how they pay for the build-out** decides who owns the upside. Shares sold dilute me; debt has to be serviced from contracts; secured loans put the GPUs up as collateral. In October the market started charging for it: CoreWeave's bonds yielded ~15–16% and Firmus pulled its IPO. My [transition rule](../weekly-reviews/_checklist.md) counts that as one confirmed signal.
