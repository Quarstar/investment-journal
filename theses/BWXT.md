## Why I'm looking at it
BWX Technologies makes the nuclear reactors and fuel for US Navy submarines and aircraft carriers. Most of its revenue comes from US government work, under long contracts that don't depend on AI spending. In Q2 2026 its backlog rose about 40% to $8.4B (book-to-bill 1.7×), and revenue grew about 18%. On 1 Oct 2026 it won a $189M naval nuclear fuel contract.

It also has nuclear upside: commercial nuclear components, and the BANR microreactor, chosen on 5 Oct 2026 for a Canadian transportable nuclear power project. That's a bonus, not the reason it's here.

## Why it replaces AVAV as my defence idea
- **Steadier customer:** Navy reactor programmes run for decades. AVAV's drone demand swings with conflicts and budget cycles.
- **Near-monopoly:** BWXT is effectively the only supplier of naval nuclear reactors.
- **Fits the energy theme without depending on it:** the nuclear know-how links to my power thesis, but the revenue comes from the Navy.

## Why it isn't a core AI name
CEG sells power to hyperscalers today at ~20× forward earnings. BWXT's AI link is microreactors that are years from meaningful revenue, at ~29×. As a core name it would just be a second nuclear bet beside VST.
