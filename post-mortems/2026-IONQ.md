| Revolut ISA | **+38%** | Averaged down, kept most of it, sold into the May–June rebound |

## What happened
IONQ fell roughly 40% from my first entries, bottomed around late March, then more than doubled off the low by June.

## Was it the decision or the outcome?
- **The averaging down was the same in both accounts.** So averaging down alone doesn't explain the loss.
- **The difference was the exit.** In the general account I had no exit plan and sold when it hurt most. In the ISA I kept holding.
- **I shouldn't learn "averaging down works" from the ISA.** IONQ happened to recover. If it hadn't, both accounts would have taken the bigger loss, and I'd doubled my exposure to a speculative name across two accounts without tracking the combined size.
- **Without a pre-set plan, my exit was driven by emotion.** Same information, opposite actions in two apps.

## What changes in my process
1. **Decide the exit before the entry**, and place the stop as a real order on swing trades.
2. **No averaging down on swing or speculative trades.** Adds are only allowed on core holdings with an intact thesis and room under the cap.
3. **One position, one view.** I track total exposure per stock across all accounts in the weekly review, so I can't double up by accident.
